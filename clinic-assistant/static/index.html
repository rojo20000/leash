Your day at the front desk

  1. Open Schedule. Everyone due today is listed by time.
  2. When someone arrives, press Check in, confirm their date of birth and insurance card, then check them in. The clinician sees them as waiting straight away.
  3. Book new appointments on the right. Only open times can be picked.
  4. Switch to tomorrow to prepare reminders. Copy each one into your usual texting or portal tool, then mark it sent.

What you can see

Names, dates of birth, contact details, insurance and appointment times. Clinical records are not available to the front desk, and the front desk assistant only knows the schedule. Clinical questions go to a nurse or clinician.

How this works

  1. Open a patient. The list starts with whoever has the most to check.
  2. Ask about them in the panel beside the record, or tap one of the suggested questions. Names and contact details are taken out before the model sees anything.
  3. Read the label on the answer before you use it.

              Patients through Leash

              Records here come from Leash's synthetic records system through the Leash gateway. Leash applies one role policy (a scheduling assistant) to every Clinic Assistant role, so the scheduling view is the most it will return and a full-chart request is blocked by design. Leash's behaviour check was trained on a scheduling agent, so patient searches are often held for review in the Leash dashboard; opening a record by its id is the direct route.
