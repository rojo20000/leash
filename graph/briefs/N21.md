## Facts you need (do not re-measure; copy numbers from files by script)
- What it is: NVIDIA NemoGuard JailbreakDetect, a classifier served at
  `https://ai.api.nvidia.com/v1/security/nvidia/nemoguard-jailbreak-detect` (the team allowed this one URL at
  14:05; see `graph/MISSION.md` UPDATE 14:05 and `CONTRACTS.md` C10.7a). Request `{"input": text}`, response
  `{"jailbreak": bool, "score": float}` (negative score = not a jailbreak). Client:
  `leash.nemotron.nemoguard_jailbreak`; model id in latency tables `nvidia/nemoguard-jailbreak-detect`.
  `LEASH_NEMOGUARD=off` drops it. Separate circuit breaker from the integrate.api.nvidia.com calls.
- Gateway: for `read_document` with no block yet, the Lightning judge (`jailbreak`) and NemoGuard (`nemoguard`)
  both run on the prescan-redacted text. TF-IDF >= 0.5 blocks (`INJECTION_DETECTED`); otherwise judge OR NemoGuard
  flag escalates (`INJECTION_SUSPECTED`, detail like `nemoguard flagged; tfidf 0.12`). Never blocks on a model
  signal alone. Decision JSON `nemotron.nemoguard = {served_by, model, flagged, latency_ms, score} | null`;
  `latency_ms.nemoguard`; `/v1/metrics` `nemotron.served_by.nemoguard`; audit `nemotron.nemoguard` and
  `nemotron.nemoguard_score` (string). Older decisions have no `nemoguard` key.
- Measured numbers: `metrics/results.json` key `nemotron` (round 5 = last entry of `eval.rounds`, note "adds
  NemoGuard JailbreakDetect as a third injection detector (team decision)"; full per-case rows in
  `ml/eval/rounds/round_5.json`). `injection` now has `tfidf`, `jailbreak` (Lightning judge), `nemoguard`,
  `combined` (all three ORed) and `combined_tfidf_jailbreak` (the rounds 1-4 definition). Rounds 1-4 have no
  `nemoguard` / `combined_tfidf_jailbreak` keys: show a dash for them.
- Honest framing (round 5, all 83 steps served live by NVIDIA): NemoGuard flagged none of the 17 evaluated
  documents, including all 8 injected ones (scores about -0.82 to -0.99, all negative); its precision and recall
  are reported as 0.0 (precision is undefined with no flags). It targets direct jailbreak prompts written to a
  chat model, not instructions hidden inside clinical documents, so it adds nothing on our document-injection
  set, and combined recall equals the two-detector figure. Live latency p50 363.8 ms, p95 662.3 ms, n 17. Say it
  is wired in by team decision as defence in depth for direct jailbreak attempts, and that it did not catch our
  indirect injections. Do not claim it improved detection.
